About GOD Kingdom Seekers Church

Learn about our vision, mission, and leadership

Our Church

GOD Kingdom Seekers Church was established in March 2020. The church is located at 5292 Phase 4 Hlalanikahle, eMalahleni Mpumalanga under Apostle AV Mkhonto.

The church is administered by the Board of Directors and the Church Council.

The church is grounded from Matthew 6:33 - "But seek first his kingdom and his righteousness, and all these things will be given to you as well."

Our Vision

  • To Portray And Proclaim JESUS CHRIST As A King Of The Kingdom Where GOD Is The Ultimate Sovereign Ruler.
  • To Build Kingdom Minded People For The Kingdom Of GOD.
  • To Make Disciples Of All Nations To Stand Right With GOD To Be Qualified Citizens Of His Kingdom.
  • To Lead And Direct People To JESUS CHRIST As He Is, "The Way, The Truth And Life."
  • To Be The Embassy Of The Kingdom Of GOD On Earth.

Our Mission

  • By Nurturing And Investing In People's Lives
  • By Training And Educating The Bride Of JESUS CHRIST To Be Ready For Eternal Kingdom
  • By Submitting To His Authority And Surrendering Our Agendas To His Divine Plan Through Prayer, Worship And Spreading Of His Word
  • By Putting GOD First In All Our Interest For The Advancement Of His Kingdom

Our Apostle

Apostle AV Mkhonto

Apostle AV Mkhonto is one of the founders and the apostle of GOD Kingdom Seekers Church.

Under his leadership, the church has grown spiritually and in numbers, focusing on building kingdom-minded believers who are committed to spreading the Gospel of Jesus Christ.

Our Church Community

Fellowship and Unity in CHRIST

At GOD Kingdom Seekers Church, we believe in the power of fellowship and unity in Christ. Our community is built on love, support, and mutual encouragement as we journey together in faith.

We are committed to building kingdom-minded people who reflect the character of Christ in their daily lives and impact their communities for the glory of God.
